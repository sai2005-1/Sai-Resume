I'm a Computer Science student at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte, concentrating in AI, Robotics and Gaming. Most of what I love about software lives at the point where code meets metal.

On a competition team, a wrong motor sign or an unclamped sensor limit costs a match. So I build modular subsystems, test them until they behave, and calibrate against real hardware — then debug in the pit when something still goes sideways.

Alongside robotics, I've shipped backend services in Java and MongoDB for a volunteer-records platform, validating every request cycle end to end.
